Gentle Note Support

Gentle Note is a private iPhone journal for reflection during eating disorder recovery.

Common help

  • Change the interface language in Gentle Note → Settings → App Language.
  • Review camera and microphone access in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Your content is stored only on this iPhone. The developer cannot retrieve deleted or lost entries.
  • Use Gentle Note → Settings → Export before moving to another device if you want an external copy.

Contact

Email coderappskrazel@gmail.com for support, bug reports, or privacy questions. Include the app version, iOS version, and a brief description. Do not include journal entries, meal photos, passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Safety

Gentle Note is not medical care or an emergency service. If you may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. The app’s Help & Safety section provides resources based on your iPhone region.

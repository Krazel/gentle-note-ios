# App Store compliance answers — candidate 0.8.1 (1)

These answers are based on the exact source data-flow inventory in
`docs/DATA-FLOW.md`. Re-run the inventory against the final 1.0 archive before
submission.

## App Privacy

- **Data collection:** Data Not Collected.
- **Tracking:** No.
- **ATT prompt:** None; the binary has no tracking SDK or IDFA use.
- User-created journal, library, intake, media, preferences, and trusted-contact
  data remain on device. User-directed export/share and opening Apple Messages
  are not developer collection.
- The support email receives only the address, message, and attachments a person
  voluntarily sends outside the app.

## Category and medical-device declaration

- Primary category: **Health & Fitness**.
- Secondary category: **Lifestyle**.
- Regulated medical device: **No**. Gentle Note does not diagnose, treat,
  calculate risk, measure health signals, monitor adherence, connect to a
  medical device, or use HealthKit.

## Age rating questionnaire recommendation

- Parental controls / age assurance: No.
- Unrestricted web access: No. Explicit support links open externally; there is
  no browser or arbitrary navigation inside the app.
- User-generated content, messaging/chat, social media, advertising: No in the
  App Store questionnaire sense; private local text/media is not published or
  shared with an in-app audience.
- Health or wellness topics: Frequent.
- Medical or treatment information: Infrequent, because Help & Safety includes
  emergency and care-seeking information while the core app avoids treatment
  guidance.
- Mature/suggestive themes: None in developer-provided content.
- Violence, substances, sexuality/nudity, gambling, contests, loot boxes: None.
- Expected Apple global result on iOS 26+: **13+**; older operating systems:
  **12+**. Save the questionnaire result generated by App Store Connect as the
  final authority.

## Content rights and encryption

- Third-party content rights: The app does not display licensed third-party
  content. Interface assets are code-native, SF Symbols, Apple system fonts, or
  developer-owned artwork. User-selected media remains the user’s content.
- Export compliance: `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO`. Local AES-GCM uses
  Apple CryptoKit and is treated as exempt standard encryption; no proprietary
  algorithm or non-exempt documentation is declared.
- License agreement: Apple Standard EULA; no custom EULA and no visible IAP in
  this candidate.

## Confirmed account-level status

- Digital Services Act: App Store Connect currently identifies the developer
  as a trader for this app.

## Decisions that cannot be inferred

- Final countries/regions of availability.
- Whether the current app icon is accepted as the public 1.0 icon.
- Final clinical and lived-experience approval of prompts, safety copy, store
  claims, and screenshots.

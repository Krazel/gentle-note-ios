# Content sources and adaptation notes

Reviewed on 2026-08-31. Gentle Note uses short, qualitative prompts inspired by
documented psychological exercises. It does not reproduce a treatment protocol,
diagnose, score, prescribe, or claim that a template produces a clinical outcome.
The wording remains subject to specialist and lived-experience review before a
public release.

## Journal templates

| Gentle Note template | Documented basis | Product adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gentle Check-In | WHO, *Doing What Matters in Times of Stress*: noticing and naming thoughts and feelings; CCI, *Break Free from ED*, Module 4: self-monitoring context, thoughts and feelings | A non-quantified check-in about what happened, what was noticed, needs and support. |
| A Balanced Thought | NHS Every Mind Matters, *Thought record*; CCI depression resources, balanced-thinking worksheets | Keeps situation, initial response, evidence for/against and a balanced or neutral view; no score. |
| Self-Compassion Pause | CCI, *Building Self-Compassion*: compassionate thought diaries and moving from self-criticism to self-kindness | Brief prompts for difficulty, common humanity and kinder words. |
| Values Compass | WHO, *Doing What Matters in Times of Stress*: acting on values | Identifies what matters and one small values-consistent action without a numerical goal. |
| Present-Moment Reflection | WHO, *Doing What Matters in Times of Stress*: grounding and engaging with the present | Notices surroundings, body sensations and what feels steady; no forced breathing or body-change instruction. |
| Prepare to Talk | US Department of Veterans Affairs visit-preparation guidance and AHRQ QuestionBuilder | Organizes concerns, desired support and boundaries before speaking with a trusted person or care team. |
| Notice Something Small | CCI self-esteem resources: Positive Qualities Record / Positive You Journal; CCI behavioural-activation material on noticing activity and achievement | Records a small act of care, honesty, flexibility or courage without ratings, streaks or achievement pressure. |

## Intake guide

The guided questions in Intakes are adapted from the context/thoughts/feelings
parts of CCI's *Break Free from ED* Module 4 self-monitoring form. Gentle Note
deliberately omits calories, weight, amounts, timing targets, behaviour counts,
weekly scores and required meal logging. It asks about context, thoughts,
feelings or sensations, what felt difficult or supportive, needs, and anything
the person may want to remember or share.
